## Design and history

[The 24-area specification](mega-city-one-spec.md) defines the design/acceptance
contract; it is not a completed checklist. [The initial live trial](live-trial.md)
and [standard-only boundary](standard-theme-boundary.md) are historical stage
records. They do not override subsequent authorisation of optional companions.
Historical manifests record their collection/promotion event, not today's entire
source state. The [build log](../harness/build-log.md) retains observed commands,
failures and subsequent corrections; current guides do not require reading that
chronology to understand installation status.
